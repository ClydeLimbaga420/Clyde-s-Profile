Academic Project

Volcanic Forecast Simulation

An interactive hazard-mapping simulation covering 25 Philippine volcanoes, rendered over live satellite imagery. Models PHIVOLCS-style alert levels, earthquake magnitude, and wind-driven ash plumes using an inverse-square/exponential damage model and a physically-based Gaussian plume that shifts and elongates with wind speed. Live stats (hazard zone size, ash-fall area, max downwind reach) update as you adjust the controls.
